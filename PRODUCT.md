# Product: Royal Henna by Neha website

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), hosted on GitHub Pages
- **Live:** https://10elizabethbell.github.io/royalHennaByNeha/

## Users
- **First:** Neha, the artist (owner). She sees the link in a Facebook message on her phone. The site has to look like her business, done better than she pictured, on the first screen.
- **Then:** people booking henna for an occasion: brides and their families, party hosts, people going to festivals. They find her through Facebook group posts and Instagram, on phones, often inside Facebook's in-app browser. *(Inferred from the occasions she lists and where she posts.)*

## Purpose
Give a business that lives in two flyer posts and an Instagram account a real home page, and turn "message me" into a booking: occasion, date, place and headcount, sent straight to her WhatsApp.

## Positioning
Henna for any occasion across NJ, NYC, Philly and PA, drawn by Neha herself. "All Occasions Accepted" (her words).

## Capabilities
- Book on WhatsApp (+1 732-498-7248, printed on her flyer) with a pre-filled message
- Call the booking line 313-979-6757 (tap-to-call in the header)
- Short "plan your henna" form that writes the WhatsApp message for the visitor; plain SMS to the booking line as a fallback
- Link to her Instagram work (@royal_henna_by_neha, checked live 2026-10-18: 631 posts, 358 followers)

## Constraints
- No home or studio address published; do not show one (brief). 313 is a Michigan area code against an NJ/NY/PA service area, so the site doesn't state where she is based.
- No prices, reviews, hours, years in business or email: say nothing about them.
- No work photos available without an Instagram login; no stock photos of other artists' henna.
- No Facebook business page found; posts come from a "Royal HennaArtist" profile in groups.
- Must be light and work in Facebook's in-app browser on cellular.

## Brand commitments
- Name: **Royal Henna by Neha** (flyer: ROYAL HENNA BY NEHA).
- No logo file. The profile picture/flyer sets "ROYAL" in a black serif over cream with henna-brown work; the brief mentions pink/red/purple on the flyer (unverified).
- Tone: warm, celebratory, first person ("I"), one artist.

## Evidence on hand
- Brief from Muse (2026-10-19 run): services/occasions, phone, WhatsApp, area, Instagram handle.
- Instagram profile live (verified 2026-10-18), profile picture shows the same numbers.

## Principles
1. Phone first: the first screen says who, what, and how to book.
2. Only real facts; gaps go to HANDOFF.md, not onto the page.
3. The page itself is made of henna: the visitor can pipe paste with a finger and watch it stain.
